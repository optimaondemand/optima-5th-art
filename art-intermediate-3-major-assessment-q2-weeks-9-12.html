Design Proposal: Ancient Evidence, Original Choice
Apply the skill in a new way.
Part 2

Plan an original object or flat design inspired by ancient evidence. Choose one focus: communication, identity, daily life, ceremony, or belief. You may use pattern, symbol, object shape, or material ideas, but your final idea must be your own.

Photo reminder: Take a clear photo of your design/artwork in good light. Make sure the whole page is visible. Upload it in Canvas with your typed responses.
Which ancient detail inspired you? What did it show about purpose or meaning?
Describe your original design. What will it be used for or communicate?
What did you borrow as inspiration, and what did you change to make the design your own?
How can art serve as evidence of what mattered to a culture?

Self-Check Before Submission
